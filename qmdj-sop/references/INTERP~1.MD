## Significator selection

Select roles before reading outcomes.

| Domain | Primary | Secondary |
|---|---|---|
| General question | day stem: questioner | hour stem: immediate event/counterpart; chief gate: process |
| Career/approval | day stem | Open Gate, authority's explicit stem if known, hour stem |
| Product/project | day stem/team | hour stem/execution object, Open Gate/approval, Scenery Gate/presentation, Life Gate/growth |
| Finance | day stem | Life Gate, Wu for capital where lineage supports it |
| Health | patient stem | Tian Rui/illness, Tian Xin/treatment, Life Gate/vitality |
| Documents/exams | day stem | Scenery Gate, Tian Fu, Ding |
| Cooperation | both parties' selected stems | Six Harmony and chief gate |
| Travel | traveler stem | target direction, Open/Rest gates, horse star |

Gender-based shortcuts are lineage-dependent and should not override the day/hour-stem structure without explicit justification.

## Reading order

1. Check Fu Yin, Fan Yin, chief star, and chief gate.
2. Read the questioner palace.
3. Read the counterpart/target palace.
4. Read the matter or decision channel.
5. Compare palace Five-Phase relationships.
6. Read gate, star, spirit, and stem combination.
7. Apply void, tomb, oppression, punishment, and horse star.
8. Translate into testable hypotheses.

## Symbol boundaries

Symbols describe a possible pattern, not a person's hidden mental state.

Examples:

- Tai Yin may support a hypothesis of privacy, indirectness, hidden information, or quiet support. It does **not** prove secret affection.
- Six Harmony may indicate cooperation, linkage, negotiation, or dependency. It does **not** prove a relationship will form.
- Death Gate may indicate closure, inactivity, exhaustion, or non-growth. It does **not** guarantee literal death or rejection.
- Injury Gate may indicate friction, criticism, cost, competition, or emotional sensitivity. It does **not** prove malicious intent.
- Xuan Wu may indicate opacity or incomplete information. It does **not** prove deception.

Tie each interpretation to observable evidence that could confirm or refute it.

## Modifiers

### Void

Treat void as reduced substance, delay, absence, uncertainty, or failure to manifest. Do not automatically turn every unfavorable symbol into good fortune. Ask what is absent in reality.

### Tomb

Treat tomb as containment, limited expression, unavailable capability, storage, or delay. A favorable symbol in tomb may exist but fail to act.

### Gate oppression

Treat an oppressed gate as constrained access or execution. A favorable gate can remain directionally favorable while losing force.

### Fu Yin

Expect repetition, inertia, slow progress, or unchanged conditions. Favor review, consolidation, and patience over repeated identical actions.

### Fan Yin

Expect reversals, volatility, changed instructions, or repeated rework. Favor short feedback loops and reversible commitments.

### Horse star

Expect movement, travel, reassignment, rapid change, or a need to act. Horse alone does not mean the action will succeed.

## Domain applications

### Product and organizational diagnosis

Map symbols into hypotheses about:

- user value;
- product scope;
- engineering feasibility;
- evidence quality;
- decision criteria;
- ownership and dependencies;
- privacy and compliance;
- stakeholder incentives.

Then test them with research, telemetry, technical spikes, risk reviews, and pre-alignment. Never advise shipping, canceling, reorganizing, or escalating solely from the chart.

### Interpersonal questions

Use reciprocity as the reality anchor. Strong evidence includes repeated initiative, specific plans, follow-through, and consistent investment. Weak evidence includes likes, views, emojis, politeness, and ambiguous delays.

Do not encourage repeated pursuit when the user reports repeated cold responses or non-reciprocity. Recommend boundaries and a single clear, respectful invitation at most; after that, let observable action decide.

### Date selection

Evaluate each candidate at its actual local date and hour. Use the same school and timezone conversion. Shortlist candidates only after excluding core mismatches and checking the relevant significators, not merely the chief gate.

## Anti-patterns

Do not:

- choose significators after seeing favorable palaces;
- mix lineages;
- call every Tian Peng a romance star;
- interpret every Tai Yin as secret love;
- treat void as always positive;
- claim precise probability without empirical calibration;
- produce categorical promises;
- overwrite inconvenient real-world facts with symbols;
- repeatedly cast until a favorable answer appears.
